# Red_College ("Red's")

A static dashboard of first-year undergrad application deadlines and requirements for ONE applicant ("Red"): a US and Canadian dual citizen and California resident, attending a US high school (US curriculum, AP, SAT/ACT available), who has never lived in Canada and wants to study pre-veterinary. Fall 2027 entry. It is a girly, frilly page behind a joke password gate.

Live site (GitHub Pages): https://deadlines.chickenkiller.com/ (also https://ben77g.github.io/Red_College/). Repo: https://github.com/ben77g/Red_College (public). README last fully rewritten 2026-10-09.

## Quick start for a future session
- Everything lives in `index.html` (no build step, no dependencies). Edit it and push to `main`; GitHub Pages redeploys in about a minute.
- The picture-frame border is generated: edit `make_frame.py`, run `python3 make_frame.py` (rewrites `frame.svg` and the frame CSS in `index.html`). Never hand-edit the frame CSS.
- Always render and look at the result before saying a change worked (see "Testing"). The assistant cannot view images itself, so screenshots are sent to the owner to check.
- The owner said to stop messaging claude cloud sessions ("Red makeover" was the one used). Do not start or message cloud sessions unless the owner asks again.
- The footer says "Last updated 2026-10-09". Update that date when the data changes.
- Keep this README current: update the matching section whenever you change something.

## How the page works
**Data.** `SCHOOLS` is an array in the script. Each school has `id`, `name`, `place`, `country`, `platform`, `fee`/`feeUnv`, `you` (the "For you" text about this applicant)/`youUnv`, `tests`/`testsUnv`, `docs` (list), `url`, `extra` (notes), and `deadlines` (`l` label, `d` ISO date, `u:1` if unconfirmed). Helper objects keyed by school id: `BRAND` (icon abbreviation and colors), `TARGETS` (date used by the progress bar and the caution rule), `TASKS` (checklist steps) and `PREVET` (pre-vet box). To add a school, add an entry in all five. Current schools (11): McGill, University of Toronto (St. George), UBC Vancouver, UCLA, Dalhousie, McMaster, Guelph, University of Alberta, UVic, University of Calgary, SFU. Icons are colored monogram badges, not official logos.

**Layout.** No visible header (a hidden h1 stays for screen readers). A short note box explains "unconfirmed" and the tape. Left: all upcoming deadlines across schools (date chips, days left, color by urgency; clicking one opens that school). Right: one icon per school. Clicking an icon expands it to full width; click again to close. Country filter and search box above. The open school shows: the platform line, the meadow progress bar and checklist, a "For you" box, two columns (left: Deadlines, Application fee, Notes; right: Standardized tests, Documents and extras), then a full-width green pre-vet box (two columns inside), then the official-page link. Everything stacks on narrow screens. The footer shows only "Last updated <date>".

**Theme.** Girly and frilly: pink background with faint hearts and dots, script headings (Parisienne), Nunito body text, ribbon-labelled cards, glossy badges, a bow on each closed icon. Fonts load from Google Fonts and fall back to system fonts offline (the owner decided not to worry about a privacy policy or terms, 2026-10-09). Small icons are inline SVG data URIs.

**Progress bar (time to deadline).** Each open school has a meadow scene (`progressBlock`): sky, clouds, a sun showing the percent, an "Aug 1" signpost, a rainbow, a pink flag with the deadline date, two-tone grass and a trotting pink unicorn (`UNICORN_SVG`; legs animate unless the OS asks for reduced motion). It is time based: it fills from `SEASON_START` (Aug 1, 2026, when Common App and the UC application open) to the school's date in `TARGETS`, computed from today's date at page load. Targets: McGill, U of T, UBC, McMaster, Guelph Jan 15, 2027; UVic, SFU Jan 31; Alberta, Calgary Mar 1; Dalhousie Mar 15; UCLA Nov 30, 2026. Several are unconfirmed (see Data notes).

**Checklist and mini bars.** Below the bar is a personal checklist (`TASKS`, heart-shaped checkboxes, its own small pink bar). Ticks are saved in the browser's localStorage under `red_tasks_v1` (per browser, not shared across devices) by step position, so append new steps at the END of an array. Each closed icon shows a small pink bar (`miniBar`) with "x of y steps" from the same ticks; it refreshes when the icon closes. The checklist does not move the unicorn.

**Work-in-progress tape.** A school whose main (target) deadline is unconfirmed (a secondary source or not found) gets yellow tape reading "WORK IN PROGRESS" (black text, slanted black bars) running diagonally from the top-left to the bottom-right corner of its closed icon, plus a yellow caution sign next to its name when open. Rule: `isCaution(s)` is true when the deadline matching the school's `TARGETS` date has `u:1`. Today that flags Dalhousie, McMaster, Guelph and Alberta. Remove `u:1` once a date is confirmed and the tape disappears. The tape band is an SVG background, rotated and sized by `layoutTapes()` to match each tile. Known issue: on a phone the tape covers part of the school name (owner screenshot, 2026-10-09); thinner, see-through, or corner placement was offered, not yet chosen.

**Pre-vet box.** `PREVET[id]` has `apply` (which program to apply to), `notes` (list), optional `alt` (the small italic note about the agricultural-campus option), `unv` and `url`. `PREVET_BASICS` (typical vet-school prerequisites, experience hours, suggested high school courses) is shown in every box. Owner rule: for schools with an agricultural campus, the box focuses on a good vet-prep program at the MAIN campus and the agricultural option is only the italic `alt` note (McGill: Faculty of Science B.Sc. Biology, Macdonald Campus as the note; Dalhousie: Faculty of Science BSc in Halifax, Truro as the note). Each checklist ends with a "choose the pre-vet route" step.

**Password gate.** `#gate` covers the page until the password is entered. The figure is a one-eyed cyclops bust (replaced a dragon on 2026-10-09 because the owner found the dragon goofy; the code keeps the old names `.dragon`, `#dragon`, `breathe`, `shake`). It is one SVG (viewBox `0 0 600 760`): bald, ruddy skin, heavy brow, one big hazel-green eye that blinks (`.lid`), broad nose, wrinkles, scars, an open jaw with crooked teeth and two tusks, shoulders in a leather garment fading out at the bottom. The same `#realism` SVG filter adds muted color and bump-lit skin texture. The password box (`#gateForm`, one input, no button, no heading text) sits in the mouth (mouth interior about x 178..422, y 580..724; form at left 33.5%, top 81.5%, width 33%). Enter submits. A wrong password shakes the figure and shows a message pinned to the bottom of the screen. A right one (not case sensitive) makes the figure rumble, fire bursts out of its mouth (`.fire`, centred at left 50%, top 86% of the figure box), flames flood the screen, then the gate fades to the site. The unlock is remembered per browser tab (sessionStorage `red_unlocked`). The password is the `PASSWORD` constant in the last script. NOT secure: it is plain text in `index.html` and the repo is public. It only stops casual visitors. If the figure moves, move the form and the fire together. The old dragon is in git history (commit before "Gate: cyclops instead of the dragon").

**Room and lighting.** The figure is centred vertically (`.dragon { margin:auto 0 }`). The room is nearly black: the `#gate` background is a stone wall image under a .86 black overlay (faintly visible everywhere). Two wall torches (`.torch.l`, `.torch.r`: inline SVG with iron plate, wooden handle, three animated flame layers, rising embers) flank the figure, dimmed (brightness .7, small glow). The brighter stone (`.stone`) shows only near the torches (mask of two radial gradients, brightness .62, sepia warmth), with a faint screen-blended orange glow (`.torchlight`, alphas .2/.07). The figure is dimmed (`#gate .dragon svg` brightness .55) with warm side light (`.rim.l`, `.rim.r`, alphas .3/.1). The rim boxes are 220% wide and 140% tall with glows centred well inside, because a small box once cut the glow off in a hard orange line. Layout variables on `#gate`: `--dw` figure width, `--tc` distance from the screen edge to a torch (clamped so torches stay on screen), `--ty` torch height (42vh, 30vh on phones where the torches also shrink). Light flickers (`lightflick`) and flames stop animating if the OS asks for reduced motion. To tune: the `.86` overlay (room), `.torchlight` alphas (glow), `.rim` alphas (firelight on the figure).

**Frame (border).** A fixed `#frame` draws a detailed white picture frame with carved acanthus leaf molding (bead-and-reel row, an acanthus band with serrated veined leaves and gold darts, an egg-and-dart band, gold fillet, pearls, a cove, mitred corners under a corner block with a rosette and four leaves). It is a 3x3 SVG used as a CSS `border-image`, inlined as a data URI. The leaf is defined once in `<defs>` and stamped with `<use>`, which keeps the data URI about 36 KB (Chrome silently ignored a 400 KB one). Cells are 96 SVG units; on screen the width is `--frame` (34px). On screens up to 600px wide the frame is hidden and `--frame` is 0 (`make_frame.py` writes this rule too). Body padding matches `--frame` so content never sits under the frame. Earlier looks (gold-and-rose macrame, pearl-and-scallop) are in git history.

## Applicant profile and what it means
- US and Canadian dual citizen, California resident, US high school, never lived in Canada. The profile box was removed from the page on request; each school's "For you" box carries this.
- Canada: Canadian citizenship should give domestic status at U of T and UBC (UBC unconfirmed). SFU confirms Canadian citizens get domestic tuition anywhere. McGill: Red likely qualifies for Quebec resident tuition under Quebec "Situation 8" (a Canadian citizen who has never lived in Canada and takes up residence in Quebec; needs a sworn statement, 10-year activity proof, 3 months of Quebec residence; unconfirmed that living there only to study counts; ask McGill Student Accounts). Rates for 2026-27: Quebec $103.92/credit vs $432.85/credit out-of-province (the latter for students first enrolled in 2024-25). Source: https://www.mcgill.ca/legaldocuments/quebec/situation8. Domestic status at Dalhousie, McMaster, Guelph, Alberta, UVic and Calgary was NOT confirmed on official pages.
- US: UCLA treats a US citizen and California resident as a domestic, in-state applicant, eligible for FAFSA.
- History: the first version assumed a Canadian-only citizen who might be international at US schools; that text was removed when the dual citizenship was confirmed.

## Pre-vet findings (researched 2026-10-09)
- Pre-vet is an advising track, not a major, at every school here. None of McGill, U of T, UBC, UCLA, McMaster, UVic or SFU has a vet school.
- Guelph has the Ontario Veterinary College (OVC). Per OVC's official page the domestic DVM cohorts need 12 consecutive months of Ontario residency (not counting post-secondary study) and the international cohort excludes anyone with Canadian citizenship, including dual citizens. A California-resident dual citizen fits neither unless they move to Ontario. OVC wants 8 university courses (biological sciences, cell biology, genetics, biochemistry, statistics, humanities/social sciences), a 75% average, no early admission.
- Calgary has its own vet school (UCVM), graduate-entry, requiring Alberta residency and Canadian citizenship or permanent residency, so no direct path for Red unless Alberta residency is established. Alberta's pre-vet option (ALES Animal Science, Pre-Vet concentration) is from a third-party snippet.
- WCVM (Saskatchewan) may admit a dual citizen through non-subsidized or international seats (unconfirmed; wcvm.admissions@usask.ca). UBC's Applied Animal Biology (Land and Food Systems) prepares students for WCVM.
- AVC (UPEI, near Dalhousie): at least 20 university courses (animal biology, genetics, statistics, three chemistry including organic, English composition), 75% average; 54 subsidized seats for PEI/NS/NB/NL residents, 41 unsubsidized seats for other Canadians and international applicants; a dual citizen uses the international pool and pays international tuition.
- McGill: Faculty of Science B.Sc. Major in Biology (Program Group: Biological, Biomedical and Life Sciences). US applicants need pre-calculus plus two of biology, chemistry or physics; SAT/ACT optional. Its Macdonald Campus Animal Health and Disease route (Edinburgh, Ross, St. Matthew's agreements) is the italic note. The Biology Major's credit count (59) and Freshman-year details are unconfirmed (catalogue pages would not load).
- Dalhousie: no official Halifax pre-vet page, so the Halifax route (Science BSc, Biology or Biochemistry and Molecular Biology) is an inference. Truro's Pre-Veterinary Medicine pathway (75% average, Academic English 12, Pre-calculus 12) is the note.
- Typical vet-school prerequisites: a year each of general chemistry, organic chemistry and biology with labs, plus biochemistry, physics, genetics, statistics, English; UC Davis lists a 180-hour experience minimum.

## Testing (how changes were checked)
No test suite. Render with headless chromium (installed as `chromium` on NixOS), for example:
`chromium --headless --no-sandbox --disable-gpu --virtual-time-budget=3000 --screenshot=/tmp/shot.png --window-size=1300,1300 file:///path/to/copy.html`
Make a temp copy of `index.html` first: remove `class="locked"` from `<body>` and hide the gate (`style="display:none"` on `#gate`) to skip the password, and change `let openId = null;` to a school id to render it open. Add `--enable-logging=stderr --dump-dom` and grep for `Uncaught` to catch script errors. To test the gate, append a script that sets `#gatePw` and calls `form.requestSubmit()`. Headless chromium will not go narrower than about 500px, so true phone widths need the owner's screenshots. Send screenshots to the owner (the assistant cannot view images itself).
Lessons: a `border-image` data URI of about 400 KB is silently ignored; umich.edu blocked page fetches during research; a CSS gradient box that is only slightly bigger than its element will show a hard edge.
